I am on a mission to make vibe coding to feel more like engineering, without losing the speed and joy of creating.
What I'm building:
Percept: Give your apps eyes
I'm building Percept to make computer vision easier to integrate into real applications. Instead of writing a new CV pipeline for every idea, tell a fixed camera what you want to watch for. Percept handles when to look, when to call a vision model, and when to send an actionable event to your app or agent.

The bigger goal: go from “I wish my app could see this” to a working integration in minutes, not weeks.

Open-source runtime (the basics built) · Percept Cloud (in development)

Tools I'm building along the way
DAD: Diagram as deliverable. Understand your code through architecture diagrams that are tightly coupled with your codebase.

Shush: Keep your secrets safe.
A local-first macOS secret manager with a CLI for people and coding agents. Because API keys shouldn't end up scattered across downloads, project files, and chat windows.

Dispatch Board: Your personal engineering manager.
A Claude Code-native way to plan work, dispatch what's ready, track progress, and bring the important decisions back to you.

Most of these started with something I wished existed while building.
